type: improvement
section: Downloads
---
The download history on the Activity page now folds a batch of chapters from one series into a single row, such as "Ch. 109–119, 11 chapters". Open the row to see each chapter as a chip that opens it in the reader; a dashed chip came from a different source. You can filter the history by Completed, Cancelled or Upgrades, search it by series, and switch grouping off to get the old one-row-per-chapter list back.
