section: Discover

Personal recommendations on Discover and Home's "You might like" are better at finding what you would actually go on to read. Maki now also weighs what other readers who finished the series you finished went on to finish, from a table it downloads in the background like its other recommendation data, about 13 MB. Picks lean a little more toward well-known series than before. A pick this brought in says "Readers who finished X also finished this". The series page's "More like this" is unchanged.
