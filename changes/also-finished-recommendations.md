type: improvement
section: Discover
---
Personal recommendations on Discover and Home's "You might like" are better at finding what you would actually go on to read. Maki now also weighs what other readers who finished the series you finished went on to finish, from a table it downloads in the background like its other recommendation data, about 13 MB. A pick this brought in says "Readers who finished X also finished this" when one of your series accounts for a large part of it, and "Readers like you also finished this" when many of your series each nudged it a little. The same rule now applies to every "Fans of" card, so a card no longer names a series that only nudged the pick. The series page's "More like this" does not use this table.
