---
type: feature
section: Chapter notes
---
Write notes on any page of a chapter while you read.

- A note is private or shared with everyone who can see the series. Shared notes take replies, and a reply notifies everyone in the thread through the inbox.
- On wide screens the notes for the page in view sit in the margin beside it. In the scrolling strip each page's notes line up with that page. Without room for a margin, a page with notes gets a tab on the screen edge, and phones use a bottom sheet.
- Other people's shared notes stay folded until you finish the chapter, so they can't spoil it.
- The toolbar's notes button lists every note in the chapter. On the series page, a chapter row shows its note count and opens the same list.
- "Notes beside the page" in the reader settings turns the margin off per reading profile. Admins can moderate shared notes.
