---
type: feature
section: Series page
---
The chapter table can group chapters under collapsible volume headers. Each header shows the chapter range, how many are downloaded, read and unread, and has its own Want switch, a download button for the volume's missing chapters and a menu to mark the volume read or unread. Chapters with no volume go under a "No volume" header at the end. Grouping turns on by itself when most chapters have a volume, and the "Group by volume" switch in the table's view menu overrides that, remembered in your browser. It is off while sorting by release date or while anime seasons are marked in the table.
