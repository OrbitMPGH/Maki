type: improvement
section: Look and feel
---
Colours now mean one thing each, and the same thing has the same colour on every screen:

- Your accent colour marks what is yours: read chapters, your bookmark and streak, and series in your library.
- Green means done, blue means something is happening, amber means it is waiting on you, red means it failed or removes something, and grey means there is nothing to report. Downloads, series status, trackers and file problems all follow this, so a missing file is amber everywhere and a queued download is grey everywhere.
- Volume pills are dusty mauve everywhere, anime is violet, and scores are gold.
- Content ratings have their own scale from cool to hot (Safe teal, Suggestive lime, Erotica orange, Pornographic magenta), so Pornographic no longer looks like an error and Erotica no longer matches Hiatus.
- Release quality reads like medals: Official gold, Scanlator silver, Aggregator bronze.
- Charts only use a status colour when the bar or slice really is that status.
