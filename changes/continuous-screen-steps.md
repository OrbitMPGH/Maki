type: feature
section: Reader
---
Continuous mode no longer needs constant scrolling. With tap zones on, a tap on the bottom third of the screen moves the strip down most of a screen and a tap on the top third moves it back, leaving a strip of what you just read at the top so you keep your place. Space, Page Down and Page Up do the same on a keyboard. There is also an auto-scroll button in the toolbar (or press A) with ten speeds: it pauses while your finger is on the screen or the toolbar is up, picks up again after you scroll by hand, and carries on into the next chapter when auto-advance is on.
