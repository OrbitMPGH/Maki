section: Discover
---
Discover's Browse tab is redesigned to help you pick something to read:

- **Start here** replaces the rotating hero: one pick from your recent reading and one each from the other personal sections, each with its synopsis, and none of them repeated further down. "Show other picks" brings the next three. Without reading history it shows the popular titles of a genre you choose.
- **Based on your recent activity** works as before, and pressing one of your series above it narrows the row to picks from that series alone.
- **Another side of your library** shows each smaller theme on your shelf as a card, side by side.
- **Readers like you also finished** has a column per group of readers you were matched with, showing how many of them finished each pick. Each group opens on its own tab in the full view.
- **Trending now** is a ranked chart (the top ten, more on a wide screen) with how many places each title climbed this month.
- **Browse the catalogue** opens with four ways in (short and finished, long and finished, new this year, top rated), a row of genres and the catalogue filters, applied to the grid in place.
