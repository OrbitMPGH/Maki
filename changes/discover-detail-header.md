section: Discover

The Discover detail card's header takes much less room, so the synopsis, tags and reviews start higher:

- Long titles stop at three lines and get smaller as they get longer. The full title shows on hover.
- The header is laid out like Home's Continue reading hero, and like the series page. When you open a pick from a recommendation rail, the reason the card gave (such as "Feels like ..." or "Close to your taste") sits above the title. Under the title are one alternative title and a line with the year, the chapter count and the volume count.
- Status, content rating and type sit together as pills, with a small TV mark on the type when there is an anime adaptation. How long the series would take you at your usual reading pace is a short line beside them.
- The score says where it stands, not just what it is: "8.6 Exceptional", then "Top 1% of popular manhwa" against the most popular fifth of the same type. Scores of 8.5 and up are Exceptional, 8.0 Great and 7.0 Good; below 7 there is no word, only where it stands.
- Each site is one chip with its score inside, rated sites first, genres moved under the synopsis, and other alternative titles are listed under At a glance.
- Adding is one card that works like the series menu: Folder and Incognito show their current setting and open a page of choices that explain themselves (each folder with its free space), Automation holds monitoring and the quality profile, Add to library comes last with Preview first chapter under it, and shortlist and feedback sit along its foot. Its deeper pages open over the synopsis below, so the header keeps its height.
- The header now scrolls away with the rest of the card. Once the add card is out of sight, a slim bar keeps the title, score and its word, status and the add button at the top.
