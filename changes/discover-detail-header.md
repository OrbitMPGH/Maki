section: Discover

The Discover detail card's header takes much less room, so the synopsis, tags and reviews start higher:

- Long titles stop at three lines and get smaller as they get longer. The full title shows on hover.
- Status, content rating and type sit together as pills, with a small TV mark on the type when there is an anime adaptation. The score is a ring, next to the chapter count, the first release year and how long the series would take you at your usual reading pace.
- Each site is one chip with its score inside, genres moved under the synopsis, and other alternative titles are listed under At a glance.
- Adding is one card: folder, quality, monitoring and incognito as rows, Add to library last, and preview, shortlist and feedback along its foot.
- The header now scrolls away with the rest of the card. Once the add card is out of sight, a slim bar keeps the title, score, status and the add button at the top.
