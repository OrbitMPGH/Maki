section: Discover
---
The pick at the top of Discover now uses the same header as the detail view that opens from it: the reason it was picked above the title, one line with the year and length, status, content rating and type as pills, the score on one line with its standing, and a chip per site with its score.

Scores look the same across the app: a gold star and one decimal ("8.0", not "8") in the Library table, the shortlist, reviews and Discover list rows. Your own rating in the Library table has a star in your accent colour, so it no longer looks like the community score beside it. The rating stars you click use the same gold as every other score. On the series page, "Open on" shows the same site chips as Discover.
