The Your taste tab is redesigned and has more to show:

- It opens on your three biggest themes with their covers, the tags you read most and avoid, and buttons to recommend from your taste or manage your signals.
- Recurring themes are a list beside a panel that shows each theme's series and picks without moving the page. The odd one out has a "More like it" button, and "Outside your core" names the side tastes the themes miss.
- From your own shelf picks the unread and paused series you already own that fit what you read best, with the reason and a button to start reading.
- How you read shows where you usually stop, with the series you put down pinned to the chart, how long the series you finish are against the ones you stall on, your pace for paged and long-strip series apart, and whether you binge a series or follow it over weeks. "Finished, under N chapters" now uses your own range.
- Where your taste has moved is one card per quarter with its tags and covers.
- What you reach for shows the genres and tags you read more than your shelf or the catalogue predicts, how deep into the catalogue's popularity ranking you read, and what you collect but rarely open, each leading to more to read.
- What shapes your recommendations moves to the end of the tab and adds the series that steer your picks most, with Recommend from this and Exclude from taste, what became of the series you added from recommendations, and which genres your anime list pulls your profile toward.

The genre, format and creator counts stay on the Stats page.
