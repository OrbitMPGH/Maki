---
type: feature
section: Reader
---
The reader can leave out scanlator credit, recruitment, donation and notice pages at the start and end of a chapter. Turn on "Hide credit pages" in the reading settings. Maki looks for pages that repeat at the same edge of nearby chapters, so it works for any series without setup, and it also skips a group's banner when it is joined to the top of a webtoon slice. Maki reads the text on those pages to tell them apart, so only scanlator pages are left out: copyright pages, "localized by" and "to be continued" cards, title pages and covers stay. Your files are never changed, finishing the last page shown still counts the chapter as read, and the reading settings show how many pages were left out with a button to show them again.

An admin can also have Maki find the credit pages of the whole library in the background (Settings > Library > Credit pages, off by default): series being read first and new downloads as soon as they finish, so chapters open with them already left out. It never holds up a download or an import, and it pauses while anyone reads. Progress shows under Running now on the Health page, with a pause button. Published volumes are passed over unless their first or last pages carry a scanlator's credits. Once Maki has seen a group's notice anywhere in your library, it also catches it in a chapter where nothing nearby repeats it, such as one scanlated chapter among volumes.
