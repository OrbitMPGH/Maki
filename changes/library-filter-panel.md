type: improvement
section: Library
---
The Library's filter panel is easier to scan. Status, reading status, type and your most common genres are chips at the top, and every other filter is a row that shows its current value and opens its own page inside the panel, the same way the reader settings work. Genres and your tags can now be included or left out from one list, and Reset, the series count and Done stay at the bottom of the panel.
