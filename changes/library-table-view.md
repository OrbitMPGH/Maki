type: feature
section: Library
---
The Library has a third view next to Grid and List: a dense table with a small cover and the title, plus columns you pick and order yourself (status, chapters, unread, added, last read, latest release, size on disk, sources, quality profile, next check, year, author, community score, your rating and tags). Click a column header to sort by it. Your columns are kept in this browser, and the view is part of the page's link.
