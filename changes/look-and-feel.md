A broad polish pass. Secondary text is easier to read, input borders and switches are visible, nothing is set smaller than 12.5px, and the scrim behind titles on covers is lighter while keeping the text readable. Reduced motion keeps short fades and drops only movement. Notifications get an icon per kind and an unread mark that doesn't rely on colour, and wording is plainer on several settings and ops pages.
