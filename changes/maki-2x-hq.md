section: Reader
---
New server upscaling model, Maki 2x HQ. It gives crisper lines and solid blacks on both colour and black-and-white pages and looks close to Real-ESRGAN, at about twice Maki 2x's time a page and a little over half Real-ESRGAN's. It comes with Maki, so there is nothing to download: tick it under Settings > Upscaling and pick it as the reader's Enhance method. Maki 2x stays the faster choice for slower servers.
