Maki uses less baseline memory on startup and gives memory back sooner after you use it. The search and recommendation files it loads for Discover no longer stay counted in memory after they are unloaded, which kept about 85 MB lower after a few visits in testing. Memory used by upscaling, credit page scans and page conversion is returned once they go idle, the credit page caches unload after five minutes, and the upscaling and credit page models unload after three and one minutes, so the first upscaled page after a longer pause can take a moment more. Your browser also frees the memory used by in-browser Enhance shortly after you leave the reader. Database lookups Maki has not needed for 20 minutes are also let go instead of being kept for as long as Maki runs. Background page conversion, prepare-ahead upscaling and credit page scans also pause while heavier work (such as browser scrapes, active reading upscales or catalogue builds) is running, and TopManhua chapter downloads now load pages in small batches instead of all at once.
