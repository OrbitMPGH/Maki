type: feature
section: Page conversion
---
Page conversion setup is now one page. The rules and the default sit in one table, and each row has its own format and quality for black-and-white pages and for colour pages, so colour inserts can go to JPEG XL while the manga around them goes to AVIF. Clicking a target shows it on your own pages of that kind, taken from the files that row covers, with the page's size in the other formats beside it. Each row shows how big its files would end up, and the total for the library updates as you edit.

JPEG XL is a new format. Its pages are about the size of WebP's (a tenth smaller at High) and convert about three times faster. The reader shows them as they are in browsers that support JPEG XL and as WebP everywhere else, and OPDS, Komga and KOReader always get WebP pages. A CBZ file you download keeps its JPEG XL pages, which KOReader and most comic apps cannot open.
