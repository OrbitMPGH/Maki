type: improvement
section: Look and feel
---
A second polish pass across the whole app:

- Settings switches and selects change the moment you click them instead of waiting on the server, and roll back with a message if the save fails.
- The reader's "Note on page" button no longer sits on top of the page counter and the next chapter button.
- Discover's first tab is now called Browse. The tags steering your picks moved to the Your taste tab, and the phone hero is shorter.
- Creator pages show how many titles they have, how many are in your library, when they started and their average score.
- Catalogue covers mark series you already have with an accent check, so it no longer looks like the green Completed mark in your library.
- Between tablet and laptop widths the sidebar starts folded into the top bar, unless you have opened or closed it yourself.
- Achievement notifications show the badge you earned.
- On Home, Next up counts say how many chapters are unread, and Recently added counts how many arrived, so the two no longer share the word "new".
- Chapter labels read the same everywhere ("Ch. 38", "Vol. 10 Ch. 98"), section spacing and panel titles are consistent from page to page, light theme text and rating stars have more contrast, and text fields show a clear focus ring.
- The browser Back button closes the Discover detail view instead of leaving the page, and Ctrl+K opens search even while you are typing in a field.
- Fixed Settings being hard to find in the phone menu, raw formatting characters in Library list synopses, the Stats reading calendar opening on its oldest weeks, and series pages opening slightly scrolled down.
