---
type: feature
section: Enhance
---
New "Prepare chapters ahead" option under Settings, Server upscaling, off by default. While nobody is reading, the server upscales the next chapter of the series you have set to Reading and read in the last 14 days, up to 8 series per reader, so they open sharp even on a phone. Only series whose reader settings have Enhance on Always with a server model are prepared. The days, series, chapters per series and the space prepared pages may take (4 GB by default, apart from the cache size) are all adjustable. Prepared pages stay until you read the chapter, and reading always comes first: a prepared page in progress stops the moment someone opens a page.
