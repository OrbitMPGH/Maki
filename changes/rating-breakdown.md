---
type: feature
section: Series page
---
You now rate a series with words instead of stars, and ratings can carry one decimal.

- Your rating shows as its word and number on the series page ("Very good 7.8"). It opens a panel with the seven words, from Bad (3) to Masterpiece (10); pick one, then fine-tune with minus and plus. The rating saves when the panel closes. The stars are gone as a way to rate; lists still show a star and the number.
- Settings > Reading > Ratings switches between whole numbers and one decimal. AniList, MangaBaka and MangaUpdates get the decimal, Kitsu the nearest half point and MyAnimeList, which only takes whole numbers, the rounded score.
- Break it down, a page inside the same panel, scores Story, Characters, Art and Enjoyment (or your own categories) with the same words. Each one opens the next unscored category, so scoring all four takes four taps, and the categories suggest a combined score. It only suggests it; press Use to make it your rating.
- Categories can be switched on or off, weighted Half, Normal or Double, and you can add your own, from the panel or from Settings. Changing them never changes a rating you already set.
- The "How is it so far?" and "How was it?" prompts show the words as one row. At the end of a series, the word your "so far" score had is marked so one tap keeps it.
