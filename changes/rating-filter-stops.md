The minimum rating filter now moves in steps that match how scores actually spread. Most series score between 6.5 and 8, so the slider takes small steps there and skips the near-empty range below 6.5. Each step shows how much of the popular catalogue it keeps, such as "Top 9% of popular series", using the same comparison as the score on a series' detail card. Saved filters keep their old value until you move the slider.
