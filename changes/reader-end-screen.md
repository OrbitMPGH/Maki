---
type: feature
section: Reader
---
The end of a chapter now does more. On the last chapter of a series you have finished, the screen says so and asks for your final rating right there, with a one-tap "keep" if you had given it a score so far. The screen also shows about how much reading time is left in the series at your own pace (hover the page counter for the same figure and the rest of the chapter while you read). When you are caught up it gives a rough span for the next chapter, like "usually comes in 1 to 2 weeks", with how sure the estimate is, and never a date. If the next chapter finishes downloading while the end screen is open, "Read" appears without leaving the reader, and queued chapters show their download progress instead of only a count.
