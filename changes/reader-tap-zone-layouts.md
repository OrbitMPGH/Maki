Tap zones come in four layouts: Sides (the old thirds), L-shape, Kindle (most of the page goes forward) and Edges (both edges go forward, for one hand). The zone size can be changed and back and forward can be swapped, and while you adjust them the zones are drawn on the page itself. In Continuous the zone size sets the height of the top and bottom bands. Find them under While reading in the reader settings, or in a reading profile.
