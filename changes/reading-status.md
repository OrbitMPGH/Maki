type: feature
section: Library
---
Every series now has your own reading status: Plan to read, Reading, Completed, On hold or Dropped.

- A newly added series starts as Plan to read. Reading it anywhere Maki can see, in the reader, over OPDS or Komga, or through Kavita, moves it to Reading. Series you had already started are Reading after the upgrade, and finished ones are Completed.
- A series becomes Completed when MangaBaka lists it as completed and you have read every chapter its progress counts. If you switched some chapters off and read all the rest, Maki doesn't guess whether they were extras: the series page and the reader's end screen ask whether you're done with it. Neither happens while another chapter is still expected to come out.
- Set it from the series page, from a Continue reading card's menu, or for a whole selection in the Library. The Library can also filter by it.
- On hold and Dropped series leave Continue reading, and the OPDS and Komga on-deck shelves, until you read them again.
- Connected trackers get the status too: On hold is sent as paused, Dropped as dropped, and Completed as completed with the tracker's full chapter count, even when the tracker counts extras you skipped. A completed entry is never changed.
- Settings, Reading status has two optional rules: put a series on hold, or drop it, after a number of days without reading. They only count days when something was downloaded and waiting to be read.
- The Dropped list in Stats and Rewind now shows the series you dropped, instead of guessing from 60 days without reading.
