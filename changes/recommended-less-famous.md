Recommended leans less on famous titles. When your anime list is used for recommendations, a manga whose anime you finished or are watching no longer shows up there, scored or not; "Continue from the anime" can still offer it once it knows where the anime ended. Your page now holds about the same share of very popular series as your own shelf, and series that land on almost every reader's page rank a little lower, using a small table Maki downloads in the background.
