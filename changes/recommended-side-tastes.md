Recommended now makes room for the smaller parts of your taste. If a few of your series are romance webtoons while most are action manga, the page used to fill up with picks for the bigger taste. Now each smaller taste gets some picks of its own below the first ten, more the larger its part of your shelf, and when such a card names one of your series it is one from that taste. The first ten picks stay as they were.
