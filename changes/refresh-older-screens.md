---
type: improvement
section: Look and feel
---
Screens that had fallen behind the rest of the app now match it:

- Health's System checks show what needs a decision as cards at the top, every other check grouped by area below, and one-line notes instead of warning boxes.
- Your taste on Discover groups recent feedback by day, Manage signals keeps every action for a title in one menu, and recurring themes are a compact list you open one at a time.
- Settings: reader profiles, quality profiles, backups, the image cache and every integration are rows that open in place. Adding a custom source is a step-by-step dialog.
- The setup guide, create account, the metadata download screen and the offline screens share one card style, and the setup guide shows each step's progress in its rail.
- The Discover detail modal matches the series page: section icons, a compact At a glance list and the same tag chips. Reviews stay at the top on the right.
- Dialogs for importing, linking files, editing metadata, restoring series, naming tokens, rating imports, tags and custom rails share one layout, with a clear message when there is nothing to show.
- The series page shows external sites as small marks and keeps the chapter selection bar at the foot of the list.
- The archived library's list view shows what each series still keeps on disk.
- Settings > Account: password, two-factor, single sign-on, sessions and API keys are rows that open in place.
- Settings > Users & security: the users table shows roles and content ratings as pills with quieter edit and delete buttons, sign-in policy reads back as one sentence, and single sign-on opens in place. Permissions in the add and edit user dialogs are grouped.
- Delete confirmations, finding a match on import, creators you follow and the folder browser use the same dialog layout as the rest of the app.
