type: improvement
section: Look and feel
---
Several screens that had fallen behind the rest of the app now match it:

- Your taste on Discover groups recent feedback by day and shows each signal as a coloured word with an icon. Manage signals keeps every action for a title in one menu.
- Settings: reader profiles, backups and the image cache are easier to scan, and each integration is one row that opens in place.
- The Discover detail modal matches the series page: section icons, a compact At a glance list and the same tag chips. Reviews stay at the top on the right.
- Search releases, Manage tags, Rename files and Compare sources share one layout, with a clear message when there is nothing to show.
