Fixed the volume search grabbing an English release on its own for a series whose files are Japanese, which then replaced them. A release tagged in the other of Japanese and English is now ignored, and the release list shows why. A series with Japanese files only takes a release automatically when its title says it is Japanese; any other release, and any release for a series whose files are in more than one language, waits for your approval instead. The search also tries a series' original title now, and for a series in Japanese keeps going until it finds a Japanese release.
