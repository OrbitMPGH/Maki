section: Discover
---
Reviews now come from AniList as well as MyAnimeList, on the Discover detail card and in the series page's Reviews drawer. Each review shows its site's logo, AniList reviews show their headline and how many readers found them helpful, and a couple of the most liked Kitsu one-line takes sit below the full reviews. Series with only an AniList or Kitsu link now get reviews too, and a site that doesn't answer is named instead of hiding its reviews silently.
