type: improvement
section: Series page
---
The series page's actions menu no longer grows taller than the screen. It's now a short list of actions and groups (Files, Automation, Upgrades, This device) that open in place with a back row, without hover submenus. Monitor, Notify and Incognito are one-tap choices that explain the current setting, and a running refresh or rescan only spins its own item instead of disabling the whole menu.
