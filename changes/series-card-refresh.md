Series cards show more of their covers. The dark fade behind the title starts lower and takes on a little of the cover's colour, and the progress bar now sits under the chapter count. Hover the bar to see how many chapters are downloaded and read.

A new Series cards setting under Preferences picks where reading progress goes: on the ring around the status icon, or on the bar, drawn over the downloaded chapters and turning green once you have read every chapter. Automatic picks the bar if you read in Maki's own reader.

On Discover, the footer under each recommendation now has a fixed height, so a row of cards lines up, and its reasons are shorter.
