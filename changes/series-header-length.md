type: improvement
section: Series page
---
The series page header and the Discover preview put what you check first at the top. The pills come straight under the title, with the release year inside the type pill. Alternative titles moved out of the header: the series page lists them under Metadata, and the Discover preview shows the original and romanized titles under At a glance. On the series page the chapter and volume totals left the header too, since the Progress card and the Metadata card already show them. In the Discover preview the chapter and volume count sits under the pills in bold figures, a finished single chapter reads as "One-shot", and the reading estimate says "to read" instead of "to catch up".
