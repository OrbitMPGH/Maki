---
type: improvement
section: Series page
---
On wide screens the series page sits on one centred column, so the header, the tabs and the Chapters and Files tables line up on both edges instead of stretching across a big monitor. The tabs get their own row under the header. On very wide screens the header art fades out before the window edge, so the page stays centred. In the chapter table, Released now sits beside the chapter number, the chapter search sits in the Chapters heading beside Select, and in Metadata the folder path gets a full row.
