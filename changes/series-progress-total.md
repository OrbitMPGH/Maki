type: improvement
section: Library
---
Reading and download progress now count against the series' real chapter total instead of the chapters switched to Wanted, so Smart mode's few-at-a-time downloads no longer make a series look nearly done. The total is the one you set, or MangaBaka's, raised to the highest chapter your sources list. When specials are unmonitored, extras leave every count; when you keep them, they are added to the total and the Progress panel says how many. The Metadata panel now shows the total and the listed chapters split into chapters and extras.
