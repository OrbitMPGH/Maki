type: improvement
section: Library
---
Reading and download progress now count against the series' real chapter total instead of the chapters Smart mode has downloaded so far, so its few-at-a-time downloads no longer make a series look nearly done. The total is the one you set, or MangaBaka's, raised to the highest chapter your sources list. A chapter you switched off and never downloaded, like a prelude you skip, stays out of the total, and the Progress panel says how many it left out. When specials are unmonitored, extras leave every count; when you keep them, they are added to the total and the Progress panel says how many. When MangaBaka's total already counts the extras, such as 26 chapters and 3 specials against 29, or a finished series whose side stories run on after the last chapter, the chapters your sources list decide the total instead, so extras are never counted twice and switching them off takes them out. The Metadata panel now shows the total and the listed chapters split into chapters and extras.
