type: improvement
section: Library
---
Reading and download progress now count against the series' real chapter total instead of the chapters Smart mode has downloaded so far, so its few-at-a-time downloads no longer make a series look nearly done. The total is the one you set, or MangaBaka's, raised to the highest chapter your sources list. A chapter you switched off and never downloaded, like a prelude you skip, stays out of the total, and the Progress panel says how many it left out. When specials are unmonitored, extras leave every count; when you keep them, they are added to the total and the Progress panel says how many. The Metadata panel now shows the total and the listed chapters split into chapters and extras.
