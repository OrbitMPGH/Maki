---
type: feature
section: Series page
---
The series page's Details tab has a new "Your reading" panel. It shows how long you've spent reading the series in Maki's reader, your usual time per chapter compared with your other series, how long is left, when you started, and a chart of your reading time per week. It only appears once Maki's reader has timed some of your reading, so a series you read in Kavita or another app doesn't get an empty panel.
