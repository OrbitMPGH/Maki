---
type: feature
section: Series page
---
The series page has a new "Your reading" panel on the Details tab. It shows how long you've spent reading the series in Maki's reader, your usual time per chapter, a chart of your reading time per week, when you started and how long is left. It only appears once Maki's reader has timed some of your reading, so a series you read in Kavita or another app doesn't get an empty panel.
