---
type: feature
section: Settings
---
After an upgrade, Settings has a New tab listing the settings added since the version you upgraded from, so you can try them without reading the whole release notes. A new option inside an older card shows on its own, and every entry links to where it normally lives. New settings also carry a New pill on their usual tab. The tab and the pills stay until the next upgrade, and a fresh install doesn't show them.
