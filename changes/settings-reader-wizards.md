type: improvement
section: Interface
---
Settings, the reader and the wizards follow the same rules as the rest of the app.

- Search boxes are one size and all have a clear button.
- Settings: root folders are a row list, short choices such as Start page and Monitoring are segmented buttons, and the Kavita card saves everything with one Save.
- Reader: the chapter banner and hints are solid, no longer blurred, and stay readable in the Light and Paper themes. Every control in the settings panel has the same layout, and "Don't enhance" opens its own list.
- The setup guide, custom source, import and page conversion steps share one step strip, with finished steps in green and "Continue" as the forward button.
- Stats charts use the app's tooltip and colours.
