section: Interface
---
More of the interface is built from the same pieces.

- Work in progress is blue everywhere: downloads on Activity, the header popover, imports and torrents. The accent colour is kept for your own reading progress.
- Small covers in lists and tables share one look, with the same edge and the same placeholder when a cover is missing.
- Notices on the series page and elsewhere share one tinted box, and delete buttons are red wherever they appear.
- Times in the Health, Scrobble, Requests, Users and backup lists read as "3m ago" or "yesterday", with the full date and time on hover. Activity history keeps full dates.
