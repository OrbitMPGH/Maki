**Shortlist.** Save titles you might read later without adding them to the library.

- Discover has a new Shortlist tab, the series modal has a save button, and saved cards carry a ribbon. Each entry can hold a note.
- Rows show what changed since you saved a title (completed, resumed, on hiatus, cancelled) and where you saved it from.
- Adding a title to the library takes it off the shortlist. Hiding it or giving it a thumbs down removes it too, and says so. Saving doesn't count as a taste signal for recommendations.
