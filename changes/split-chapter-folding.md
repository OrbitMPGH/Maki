---
type: feature
section: Sources
---
When one source splits a chapter into parts (12.1, 12.2) and another lists it whole, Maki now compares the pages of both and, when they are the same chapter, treats the parts as that one chapter. The series no longer lists both versions, the numbering clash warning goes away once nothing else clashes, and Smart Download can use either source. Your reading position, bookmarks and notes move onto the whole chapter, and part files already downloaded go to the recycle bin. Parts whose pages don't match, such as extras or bonus chapters, stay separate. A newly added series is checked right after its sources are matched, before Smart Download starts on it.
