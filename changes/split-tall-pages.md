---
type: feature
section: Page conversion
---
Page conversion can split very tall webtoon pages into several shorter ones, which e-readers and KOReader handle far better. Turn it on in the conversion setup and pick a height: pages taller than that are cut in the blank gaps between panels where there are any, and the setup shows where the cuts land on the tallest page in your library. It applies to new downloads and, through the conversion pass, to files you already have, including ones that are already WebP or were converted before. Reading positions, bookmarks and page notes move with their page, and undoing a conversion puts them back.
