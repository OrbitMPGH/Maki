---
type: feature
section: Downloads
---
Maki now searches your indexers on its own for chapters no source has, such as a licensed series a site took down or a series with no source at all. It works like the volume release search: each series is searched at most once a week, a few at a time, and releases Maki is unsure about wait for your approval. Chapters a source still lists keep downloading from it. It is on by default and works even with the daily upgrade scan off; turn it off under Settings, Downloads, Volume releases.
