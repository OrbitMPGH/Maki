type: improvement
section: Enhance
---
Server upscaling with Maki 2x is faster: the model no longer works out the border of each tile it throws away, the next page starts while the previous one is still being saved, colour pages save in less than half the time, and machines with many cores can run up to 18 threads on it. Pages come out the same, except that colour pages are saved at a slightly higher quality, for files about a fifth larger, so pages upscaled before the update are made again the next time you read them.
