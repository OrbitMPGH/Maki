section: Enhance
---
Upscaled colour pages are now saved as JPEG XL. Dark gradients stay smooth on an OLED screen instead of breaking into blocks, files are about 14% smaller at the same quality, and the pages save slightly faster. When the browser that is reading can't show JPEG XL, such as Edge, the page is saved as WebP instead. Grey pages stay WebP, and pages upscaled before the update are kept until the cache drops them. An admin can turn it off under Settings > Server upscaling.
