section: Series page
---
Volumes in the chapter table are easier to tell apart. Each volume now starts with a taller heading, set off by a gap, that stays in view while you scroll through its chapters. A strip on the heading shows each chapter at a glance (read, downloaded, queued, failed, file missing, not downloaded, not wanted), with the counts beside it. The download button on the heading shows how many chapters it will fetch, and leaves out chapters you don't want. Chapters you don't want are no longer faded, so their text stays readable.
