section: Enhance
---
New "Whole chapter" switch under Enhance for server methods, off by default. The server keeps upscaling every page of the open chapter, ahead first and then behind, so fast reading doesn't catch up with it, and then carries straight on through the whole next chapter, so it opens already upscaled. A next chapter whose quality tier your settings skip (volumes, by default) is left alone. Leaving the chapter cancels the rest.
