## Scrobbling

![Scrobble page with a status chip per tracker, Set up trackers and Sync now buttons, an empty Needs review panel and a Recent syncs table showing each series' chapter and trackers](../../../assets/ui/pages/scrobble-top.webp)
<hr>

Sends read progress to AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, MangaBaka and MangaUpdates, whether the reading
happened in Maki, over OPDS, in KOReader or in Kavita. Your lists on those trackers can also be
imported into the library from Settings.

## Anime tie-in

For a series with an anime adaptation, Maki can start you at the chapter where the anime ended.

![Notice on Frieren's series page about which chapters each anime season covers, with Mark watched, Read and Not this anime buttons](../../../assets/ui/series/anime-notice.webp)

Home's **Continue from the anime** rail lists those series, your most recent tracker updates first,
and skips series the anime has fully caught up with. Dismiss a card, with undo, either until a new
season moves past the chapters it covers or for good.

![Continue from the anime rail of cover cards such as Frieren, Haikyu!! and Vinland Saga, each labelled with the chapter the anime ends at](../../../assets/ui/home/continue-from-anime.webp)

## Ratings

Rate a series from 1 to 10 when you finish it, or set a "so far" score while you are partway through.
Maki asks how a series is going at a few points while you read. Final ratings can be sent to your
trackers, and "so far" scores only when you turn that on. See
[Ratings and "so far" scores](/Maki/guides/trackers/ratings/).

![Rating picker set to Excellent, with named levels from Bad to Masterpiece, a fine-tune stepper, Break it down and Clear rating](../../../assets/ui/series/rating.webp)
