---
title: Chapter notes
description: Write private or shared notes on a page while you read, reply to other readers and keep spoilers folded.
---

You can write a note on any page of a chapter in Maki's reader. Notes need a connection, so they are
not available while reading offline.

## Write a note

Open the notes from the toolbar's notes button, or use **Note on page** on the page you are reading.
A note is pinned to the page you write it on. Wrap a spoiler in `||double bars||` and it shows as a
Spoiler chip that readers click to reveal.

Each note is either:

- **Private**: only you see it.
- **Shared**: everyone who can see the series sees it, and can reply.

Replies go one level deep, and each reply notifies everyone in the thread through the
[inbox](/Maki/guides/running/notifications/). A shared note can't be made private once someone else
has replied to it.

If the series is incognito, the composer warns you that a shared note tells everyone you read the
chapter.

## Where notes show

- On a wide screen, the notes for the page in view sit in the margin beside it. In Continuous mode
  each page's notes line up with that page.
- Without room for a margin, a page with notes gets a tab on the screen edge.
- On a phone, notes open in a bottom sheet.
- The toolbar's notes button lists every note in the chapter, and switches between **This page** and
  **Whole chapter**.
- On the series page, a chapter row shows its note count and opens the same list.

**Notes beside the page** under **While reading** in the reader settings turns the margin off. Like
other reader settings it is saved per [reading profile](/Maki/guides/reading/maki-reader/#reading-profiles).

## Spoilers from other readers

Other people's shared notes stay folded until you finish the chapter, so they can't spoil it. Tap a
folded note to show it anyway.

## Names

Other readers see your display name. Without one they see "Another reader", unless an admin turns on
**Show login names to everyone** under **Settings → Users & security**, in which case they see your login name.
The composer tells you which one they will see.

## Moderation

Admins can delete any shared note. A deleted note that has replies stays as "Removed by an admin" so
the replies still make sense. Private notes stay private from admins too.
