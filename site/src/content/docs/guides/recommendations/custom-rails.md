---
title: Custom rails
description: Build your own rails on Home and Discover from your library, the recommender or the catalogue.
---

A custom rail is a saved filter with a source, drawn as one row on Home or Discover. For the built-in
rails, see [Discover rails](/Maki/guides/recommendations/rails/).

## Create a rail

- Press **Add a rail** at the bottom of Home or Discover.
- Or set up a filter on Discover's catalogue, a creator page or the Recommended tab, open **Saved
  filters**, and press **Save as a rail…**. The rail starts with those filters set.

You can have up to 30 custom rails.

## The editor

- **Name.** Shown as the rail's title.
- **Show on.** **Home** or **Discover**.
- **Titles from.** The source, described below.
- **Sort by.** Options depend on the source.
- **Leave out series in your library.** Catalogue rails only, on by default.
- **Obscurity** and **Variety.** Recommendation rails only. Obscurity moves between **popular** and
  **gems**, variety between **closest** and **varied**.

Filters use the same panel as the catalogue. For genres and tags, pick **Has all of**, **Has any of**
or **Has none of**.

## Sources

| Source | Shows | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your library** | Series you already have that match the filters | Home only, since Discover is about what you do not own |
| **Recommendations** | Picks ranked the same way as the Recommended tab, with the same settings | Can be seeded with specific series. Needs the local MangaBaka database. No sort, because the ranking is the order |
| **Catalogue** | Anything in the MangaBaka catalogue that matches | Needs the local MangaBaka database |

If a source needs the database and it is not ready, the rail says **This rail needs the local
MangaBaka database, which is switched off or still downloading.** See
[The local MangaBaka database](/Maki/guides/recommendations/local-database/).

Sort options:

- **Your library:** **Recently added**, **Recently read**, **Title**, **Most popular**
- **Catalogue:** **Most popular**, **Top rated**, **Newest**, **Oldest**

## Arrange and remove rails

Use **Edit layout** on Home or Discover to move a rail or switch it off, and **Done** to save the
layout. Rail edits themselves save straight away.

Catalogue rails have a **Show more** button that opens the rail with its filters so you can narrow it
further.

To delete a rail, open its options and press **Delete rail**. Maki asks first, and the rail's filters
go with it.
