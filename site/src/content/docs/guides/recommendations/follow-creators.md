---
title: Follow creators
description: Follow an author, artist or studio, and read the New from creators you follow rail on Home and Discover.
---

Following a creator or studio adds a rail of their newest titles to Home and Discover.

## Follow a creator or studio

Open a creator or studio page, for example from a credit on a series page, and press **Follow**. The
button becomes **Following**.

The follow is tied to the role the page was opened for. Following a studio does not follow a person
with the same name, and following an artist does not follow the same name as an author.

## The rail

**New from creators you follow** appears on Discover, and on Home unless you switched that section
off. It shows catalogue titles from the last 12 months by the people you follow, newest first. Titles
you own, have thumbed, marked as seen, hidden or dismissed stay out.

- **Manage** lists the creators you follow, and is where you unfollow.
- **Show more** opens every title in the rail, with the followed creators as the filter.

If nothing new is left, the rail says **Nothing new from them in the last 12 months that you have not
seen already.** If you follow nobody, the rail does not appear.

## Notifications

When a followed creator has a new catalogue title, Maki adds a **New from** entry to your
notification bell.

- Only titles MangaBaka added after Maki's first check count. A creator's older back catalogue never
  triggers one.
- Titles you own or have given feedback on are not announced.

:::caution
A notification can be missed when a title's credits arrive in a later catalogue update than the title
itself.
:::

To stop following, press **Unfollow** in **Manage**, or **Following** on the creator's page.
