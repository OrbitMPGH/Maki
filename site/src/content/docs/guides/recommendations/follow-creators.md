title: Follow creators
description: Follow an author, artist or studio, and read the New from creators you follow rail on Home and Discover.
---

Following a creator or studio adds a rail of their newest titles to Home and Discover. This page covers how to follow someone, what the rail shows, and when Maki tells you about a new title.

## Follow a creator or studio

Open a creator or studio page. Click a credit on a series page, or open a title from a Discover rail and follow its creator from there. The page has a **Follow** button next to the name.

When you follow, the button changes to **Following**. Its tooltip reads **Show their newest titles in a rail on Home and Discover**.

The follow is tied to the role the page was opened for. Following a studio does not follow a person with the same name, and following an artist does not follow the same name as an author.

## The rail

Once you follow someone, the rail **New from creators you follow** appears on Discover, and on Home unless you have switched that section off. It shows catalogue titles from the last 12 months by the people you follow, newest first. Titles you already own, have given a thumb to, marked as seen, hidden or dismissed stay out.

The rail has two buttons:

- **Manage** lists the creators you follow. Unfollow from there.
- **Show more** opens every title in the rail, with the followed creators as the filter.

If nothing new is left, the rail says **Nothing new from them in the last 12 months that you have not seen already.** If you follow nobody, the rail does not appear at all.

## Notifications

Maki adds an entry to your notification bell when a followed creator has a new title in the catalogue. Each entry is titled **New from** the creator's name and says the title is new in the catalogue.

Notifications only cover titles that MangaBaka added after Maki's first check. A creator's older back catalogue never triggers one. Titles you already own, or have given feedback on, are not announced either.

:::caution
A notification can be missed when a title's credits arrive in a later catalogue update than the title itself.
:::

To stop following, press **Unfollow** in the **Manage** list, or press **Following** on the creator's page.
