This page lists the rails on the Discover Browse tab, in the order they ship, and what each one is built from. For how the engine ranks titles, see [How recommendations work](/Maki/guides/recommendations/how-it-works/). For the page itself, see [Discover](/Maki/features/discover/).

You can reorder rails or switch them off with **Edit layout**. Most rails have a **Show more** button that opens the full list.

## Spotlight

The large picks at the top of the page. Spotlight uses the rail built from your recent reading. When that rail has fewer than three titles, it fills from **Trending now**.

## Your taste

A short strip titled **Steering these picks**. It lists the five tags that do the most to steer your recommendations, with the share of your read series that carry each one. **Your taste** links to the Taste tab for the full breakdown.

## Based on your recent activity

Titled **Based on your recent activity**. Its subtitle reads **Because you read** and names up to three of the series it was built from.

It uses the six series you read most recently. Series in full incognito mode are skipped, and titles you have thumbed down, rated low or ignored as a seed are left out.

## New from creators you follow

Titled **New from creators you follow**. It shows titles from the last 12 months by creators and studios you follow, newest first. It stays empty until you follow someone. See [Follow creators](/Maki/guides/recommendations/follow-creators/).

## Side interests

One row for each smaller theme on your shelf. Each row is titled **Another side of your library**, followed by the theme name.

A theme needs at least two works that mark it as a core story element. It must also cover less than 35% of your library, so common themes do not qualify. Maki shows at most two rows, and a row needs at least three matching picks. With no vector index, or fewer than six series on your shelf, the section is left out.

## Readers like you

The section is labelled **Readers like you**. Its rail is titled **Readers like you also finished**, with the subtitle **From readers whose finished series look like yours.**

It is built from the series you have finished, not the ones still on your shelf, and matched against groups of readers. If no group matches, the rail is left out.

## Trending now

Titles climbing fastest in popularity right now, from the catalogue.

## Browse the catalogue

Catalogue rails with the catalogue filter. The rails are:

- **Most popular**
- **Newly released**
- **Top rated**
- **Popular manhwa**
- **Popular manhua**

These draw from the local MangaBaka database, so they stay empty until it is downloaded.

## Every genre

One tile per genre. Each tile opens that genre's most popular titles, with the title **Popular in** followed by the genre name.

## Next steps
