The rails on the Discover Browse tab, in the order they ship. For how the engine ranks titles, see
[How recommendations work](/Maki/guides/recommendations/how-it-works/), and for the page itself,
[Discover](/Maki/features/discover/).

Reorder or switch off rails with **Edit layout**. Most rails have a **Show more** button that opens
the full list.

## Spotlight

The large picks at the top, taken from the rail built from your recent reading. With fewer than three
titles there, it fills from **Trending now**.

## Your taste

A strip titled **Steering these picks**. It lists the five tags that most steer your recommendations,
with the share of your read series carrying each, and links to the Taste tab.

## Based on your recent activity

Its subtitle, **Because you read**, names up to three of the series it was built from. It uses the
six series you read most recently. Series in full incognito are skipped, and titles you thumbed down,
rated low or ignored as a seed are left out.

## New from creators you follow

Titles from the last 12 months by creators and studios you follow, newest first. Empty until you
follow someone. See [Follow creators](/Maki/guides/recommendations/follow-creators/).

## Side interests

One row per smaller theme on your shelf, each titled **Another side of your library** followed by the
theme name.

- A theme needs at least two works that mark it as a core story element, and must cover less than 35%
  of your library, so common themes do not qualify.
- At most two rows show, and a row needs at least three matching picks.
- With no vector index, or fewer than six series on your shelf, the section is left out.

## Readers like you

Titled **Readers like you also finished**, with the subtitle **From readers whose finished series
look like yours.** It is built from series you have finished, not ones still on your shelf, and
matched against groups of readers. If no group matches, the rail is left out.

## Trending now

Titles climbing fastest in popularity, from the catalogue.

## Browse the catalogue

Catalogue rails: **Most popular**, **Newly released**, **Top rated**, **Popular manhwa** and
**Popular manhua**. They draw from the local MangaBaka database, so they stay empty until it is
downloaded.

## Every genre

One tile per genre. Each opens that genre's most popular titles, titled **Popular in** followed by
the genre name.

## Next steps

To change what a rail shows, use a [custom rail](/Maki/guides/recommendations/custom-rails/). To
change what feeds the recommendations, see [Tune what you see](/Maki/guides/recommendations/feedback/).
