title: Stats and Rewind
description: Pick a range on the Stats page, play Rewind for a year, and keep a series out of your stats.
---

For what each section measures, see [Stats](/Maki/features/stats/).

## Choose a range

Six presets: **30 days**, **This month**, **90 days**, **12 months**, **Year** and **All time**.
**Year** adds a picker for **Whole year** or a single month.

## Sections

**Reading**, **Rhythm**, **Taste**, **Habits**, **Library** and **Progress**. **Progress** shows
levels, streaks, achievements and reading time on two tracks: **Reader** (your own reading) and
**Library** (the whole instance). Achievements stay unlocked once earned.

## Look at another reader

Admins get a **Reader** picker at the top of the page to switch accounts. Incognito reading stays
hidden even from admins.

## Play Rewind

Rewind is a slideshow of one year: pages turned, time in the reader, your busiest month, your
most-read series, the series you kept coming back to, and how much your library grew.

1. Open **Stats**.
2. Click **Play Rewind**. It stays disabled until that year has reading, downloads, or series added
   or removed.
3. Use the pause control to stop on a slide, and **Close Rewind** to leave.

Rewind always covers a calendar year: the current one, or the one picked when the range is **Year**.
Other ranges do not change it. It plays only for your own reading, so the button is missing while you
view another reader. `/rewind` redirects to the Stats page.

## Keep a series out of stats

Choose **Incognito** from the series' menu:

- **Off** records everything.
- **No scrobble** skips tracker pushes only. The series still counts in your stats.
- **Full** also leaves the series out of Rewind and reading history.

The reader toolbar has a separate **Incognito (don't record this session)** toggle for the current
session.

:::caution
Reading recorded while a series is on **Full** never reaches your history, even after you switch the
series back.
:::
