This page is the how-to. For what each section measures, read [Stats](/Maki/features/stats/).

## Choose a range

The Stats page has six range presets: **30 days**, **This month**, **90 days**, **12 months**, **Year** and **All time**. Choose **Year** to get a year picker, with **Whole year** or a single month.

## Read the sections

Stats has sections called **Reading**, **Rhythm**, **Taste**, **Habits**, **Library** and **Progress**.

**Progress** shows levels, streaks, achievements and reading time. It has two tracks: **Reader** (your own reading) and **Library** (the whole instance). An achievement stays unlocked once you earn it.

## Look at another reader

Admins see a **Reader** picker at the top of the Stats page and can switch to another account. Only admins can do this. Incognito reading stays hidden even from admins, so a series someone marked incognito never shows up for anyone else.

## Play Rewind

Rewind is a slideshow of one year. It covers pages turned, time in the reader, your busiest month, your most-read series, the series you kept coming back to, and how much your library grew.

1. Open **Stats**.
2. Click **Play Rewind**. The button stays disabled until that year has reading, downloads, or series added or removed.
3. Use the pause control to stop on a slide, and **Close Rewind** to leave.

Rewind always covers a calendar year: the current year, or the year you picked when the range is **Year**. The other ranges do not change it. Rewind plays only for your own reading. If you are viewing another reader, the button is not there.

`/rewind` redirects to the Stats page.

## Keep a series out of stats

Open the series' menu and choose **Incognito**:

- **Off** records everything.
- **No scrobble** skips tracker pushes only. The series still counts in your stats.
- **Full** also leaves the series out of Rewind and reading history.

The reader toolbar has a separate **Incognito (don't record this session)** toggle for the reading session you are in.

:::caution
Reading recorded while a series is on **Full** never reaches your history, even after you switch the series back.
:::
