This page is not legal advice.

## What Maki is

Maki organizes your library. It watches the sources you choose, downloads pages and writes them as
CBZ files into your library folder. Scraper sources connect to third-party websites, and Maki does
not check whether a site allows its chapters to be downloaded.

## Your responsibilities

- Follow the terms of service of every site Maki connects to.
- Follow the copyright and other laws where you live. Those laws decide what you may download and keep.
- If you connect an indexer through Prowlarr or Jackett, the same applies to the torrents it finds.

## Support the people who make the work

Some built-in sources are official publisher sites, such as MANGA Plus, WEBTOON and Naver Webtoon.
Others are scanlation groups. Buy official releases where you can, and read the official sources
when they carry the series you follow.
