Synopsis

Dae Ho, who became an orphan at the age of 13, was adopted by his father’s friend. However, Dae Ho in adulthood knew nothing about the relationship between men and women. Aunt and sisters decided to give pure Dae Ho a secret class…

Secret Class (also known as 비밀수업) is an ongoing Korean romance manhwa , written by Wang Kang Cheol and illustrated by Mina-chan, published by Toptoon.

It is also commonly searched as “Secret Class manga”, even though it follows the Korean manhwa/webtoon format. Readers who want to read Secret Class online in English can explore this series for its engaging plot, character-driven storytelling, and distinctive artwork.

Whether you search for Secret Class manhwa or Secret Class manga, it offers a strong reading experience within the romance genre.
